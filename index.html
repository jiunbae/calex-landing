FAQ

Good questions.

Are all conversations sent to the cloud?
No. By default, transcripts, summaries, and to-dos are stored locally on your device. Cloud engines are used only when you add your own key and turn them on.
How are sensitive conversations protected?
You can pause listening any time, or use a schedule so it only listens during certain hours. Stored data is protected by your device's disk encryption.
Can the Calex team see my data?
By default no voice or text data is sent to any server, so it can't be accessed. There's no account and no analytics tracking.
Which devices does it work on?
iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It supports Korean and English, with widgets to check recording status and recent summaries quickly.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Transcription and summarization run on-device, so core features work offline.
